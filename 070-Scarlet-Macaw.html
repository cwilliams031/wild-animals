Scarlet Macaw

Ara macao
Least Concern Lots still live in the wild
Where they live: Mexico to the Amazon
Length
About 33 in
Half of that is tail!
Weight
About 2 lb
Light for its size
Colors
3 bright ones
Red, yellow and blue
Beak
Nutcracker
Strong enough to crack nuts
Family
Mates for life
Pairs stay together
Lifespan
40+ years
In the wild

On the menu

Seeds, nuts, fruit and flowers. They also nibble clay from riverbanks.

Super power: A beak so strong it cracks open hard nuts that other animals can't.

Fun facts

  • Scarlet macaws are the national bird of Honduras.
  • They can copy sounds, even some words.
  • Flocks gather at clay licks to eat mud.
