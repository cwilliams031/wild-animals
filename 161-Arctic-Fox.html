Arctic Fox

Vulpes lagopus
Least Concern Lots still live in the wild
Where they live: Arctic tundra all around the North Pole
Weight
6.5–17 lb
About like a house cat
Coldest
–58°F
Still warm in its fur
Pups
Up to 14
In one spring litter
Paws
Furry
Fur even on the pads
Coat
Changes color
White in winter
Lifespan
3–6 years
In the wild

On the menu

Mostly lemmings and voles, plus birds, eggs, berries and leftovers from polar bears.

Super power: Super hearing! It hears lemmings under the snow, then leaps up and pounces down on them.

Fun facts

  • In summer its fur turns gray-brown.
  • Its bushy tail is a warm blanket when it curls up.
  • It's the only member of the dog family that changes color.
